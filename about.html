About

One instinct, four very different rooms.

I've built a brand from nothing and carried its P&L, as founder of TOGS-88. I've set someone else's creative and brand vision as a partner, not a founder, at KIYAM. I've walked into an unfamiliar market as the outside strategist with no prior relationship to the brand, on Belgravia Scents through Proplex. And I've stood at a trade-fair booth turning a stranger's interest into a signed order, for Home Fusion.

Four rooms, four different levels of authority, four different kinds of pressure. The instinct underneath all of them is the same: treat the brand decision, the creative decision and the commercial result as connected parts of one system — not three separate jobs handed to three separate people.

How I work
  • 01

    I follow the idea into the number.

    A creative concept isn't finished when it looks right — it's finished when I know what it did to conversion, cost per purchase, or a signed order. That's why I took TOGS's Meta advertising in-house rather than continuing to hand the follow-through to someone else.

  • 02

    I've sat on both sides of the table.

    Founder, partner, freelance strategist, and — at a trade-fair booth — the person actually closing the conversation. Each seat changes what advice I'm willing to give, because I've had to live with the consequences of the version where I was wrong.

  • 03

    I test before I claim.

    TOGS's Meta account has been mine to run since mid-August 2026. I'm not going to tell you it's been years — I'll tell you exactly what changed in that window, what I still don't know, and what I'm testing next.
