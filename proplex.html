01

The role

Unlike TOGS-88 or KIYAM, Proplex isn't one brand or one project — it's an ongoing seat inside an agency, working across whichever client brands come through in a given period. Each brief starts with someone else's business, someone else's market position, and often someone else's existing brand guidelines, rather than a blank page.

02

What the work covers

  • —

    Research and positioning — reading a client's market, competitors and existing brand before proposing a direction.

  • —

    Campaign strategy — building out the thinking behind a launch, a seasonal push, or a holiday calendar of activity.

  • —

    Creative direction — turning a strategy into an actual visual and content direction a brand can shoot and run.

  • —

    Shoot direction — on set for the resulting photography and videography, not just the deck that preceded it.

03

What I can show

Agency work is client-confidential by default — the brands, briefs and creative that come through Proplex aren't mine to publish, and this page doesn't include visuals or specifics from that client roster.

One project from this seat I can speak to in detail: Belgravia Scents, a US-market rebrand running through Proplex. It has its own case study — decisions, scoping, and what shipped.
04

What it's taught me

Working inside someone else's brand voice, on a rotating basis, is a different discipline from running TOGS-88 or partnering at KIYAM — there's no five-year context to lean on, and no shared stake in the outcome the way a founder or partner has. What it builds instead is speed at reading a brand that isn't yours: figuring out fast what a client's market actually rewards, and proposing a direction you can defend to people who didn't ask for your opinion by default.
